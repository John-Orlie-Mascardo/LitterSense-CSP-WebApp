## Defaults to review

- Registration hold: 5 seconds; missing-reader-sample tolerance: 600 ms; explicit no-tag reply resets immediately.
- Enrollment lifetime: 120 seconds. Browser poll: 500 ms. Progress interpolation capped at 95% until server verification; success visible for 1.2 seconds.
- Tag must be absent for 3 seconds before another tap is accepted. Additional debounce: 1 second.
- Each RFID-to-ultrasonic confirmation window: 5 seconds. Two fresh readings within 2-30 cm required.
- Open visit timeout: 15 minutes, recorded as incomplete with no confirmed exit.
- Ultrasonic without a prior RFID request creates no visit. A different tag cannot replace the active cat. A failed exit pair leaves the existing visit open.
- Reboot: active visit becomes incomplete; replay uses a stable identity and must be acknowledged before a new visit. Actual exit/duration are unknown and excluded from measured evidence.
